Here is your complete, crystal-clear, step-by-step master wiring guide tailored precisely to the exact ESP32 DevKit board you showed in your photo (with its VIN, 3V3, GND, and D pin labels).

Since you are powering everything directly from your laptop USB via the micro-USB port on the board, your board will distribute power through two main levels:

VIN Pin: Supplies 5V straight from your laptop USB (perfect for your LCD, Relay, Camera, and sensors that need 5V).

3V3 Pin: Supplies regulated 3.3V (for low-voltage sensors like the capacitive soil moisture sensor).

GND Pins: Your common ground. Every single ground wire from every component must eventually plug into a GND pin or the blue ground rail.

Step 0: Setting Up Your Breadboard Power Rails
Before adding any sensors, let's organize your breadboard's long side rails:

Run a jumper wire from the VIN pin on the right side of your ESP32 to the Red (Positive) Rail on your breadboard.

Run a jumper wire from the 3V3 pin on the left side of your ESP32 to a separate row if needed, or keep it ready for the soil sensor.

Run a jumper wire from any GND pin on your ESP32 to the Blue (Negative/Ground) Rail on your breadboard. This blue rail is now your Common Ground Bus.

Step 1: Subsystem A — Security Module (PIR Motion Sensor & ESP32-CAM)
1. PIR Motion Sensor
VCC Pin: Plug into the Red (Positive / VIN 5V) Rail.

GND Pin: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

OUT (Data) Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D25 on the right side of your ESP32.

2. ESP32-CAM Module
5V / VCC Pin: Plug into the Red (Positive / VIN 5V) Rail.

GND Pin: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail (crucial for sharing a common ground).

TX Pin: Run a jumper wire to D16 (labeled RX2 on your board's left side).

RX Pin: Run a jumper wire to D17 (labeled TX2 on your board's left side).

Step 2: Subsystem B — Irrigation & Water Management Module
1. Capacitive Soil Moisture Sensor
VCC Pin: Connect directly to the 3V3 pin on your ESP32 (do not use 5V here, as it prefers 3.3V).

GND Pin: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

AO (Analog Out) Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D34 on the right side of your ESP32.

2. Rain Sensor Module
VCC Pin: Plug into the Red (Positive / VIN 5V) Rail.

GND Pin: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

DO (Digital Out) Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D33 on the right side of your ESP32.

3. HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor (Water Tank Level)
VCC Pin: Plug into the Red (Positive / VIN 5V) Rail.

GND Pin: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

Trig Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D26 on the right side of your ESP32.

Echo Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D27 on the right side of your ESP32.

4. 5V Relay Module & Water Pump
VCC Pin: Plug into the Red (Positive / VIN 5V) Rail.

GND Pin: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

IN (Signal) Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D32 on the right side of your ESP32.

(Note: Connect your 12V water pump to the Relay's Common and Normally Open (NO) screw terminals with an external 12V power supply when testing physical water pumping).

Step 3: Subsystem C — Feedback & Display Module
1. 16x2 I2C LCD Display
VCC Pin: Plug into the Red (Positive / VIN 5V) Rail.

GND Pin: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

SDA Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D21 on the left side of your ESP32.

SCL Pin: Run a jumper wire directly to D22 on the left side of your ESP32.

2. Alarm Buzzer
Positive (+) Leg: Connect through a 220-ohm resistor to a free row on your breadboard, then run a jumper wire from that row to D23 on the bottom-left of your ESP32.

Negative (-) Leg: Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

3. Red LED (Intrusion Alarm)
Anode (+ / Long Leg): Connect through a 220-ohm resistor to a free row, then run a jumper wire to D13 on the top-right of your ESP32.

Cathode (- / Short Leg): Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.

4. Green LED (System OK Status)
Anode (+ / Long Leg): Connect through a 220-ohm resistor to a free row, then run a jumper wire to D4 on the upper-left of your ESP32.

Cathode (- / Short Leg): Plug into the Blue (Ground) Rail.
